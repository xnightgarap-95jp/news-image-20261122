## 1. Iran fires a new missile and drone wave at US bases in the Gulf

Early on Sunday, Iran's Revolutionary Guard fired missiles and drones at US
military sites in Kuwait and Bahrain, naming Ali Al Salem Air Base in Kuwait
and the US Fifth Fleet headquarters in Bahrain among the targets. US Central
Command and Gulf officials said almost all of the incoming weapons were
intercepted, with no US casualties and no damage to US bases. Bahrain
reported a fire at a civilian industrial facility after an Iranian strike
late on Saturday. President Donald Trump warned Iran's leaders over the
strikes.

## 2. COP31 nears a final deal in Antalya

The UN climate talks in Antalya ran past their Friday deadline, straight
through Saturday and into the early hours of Sunday. On Sunday the summit
president, Murat Kurum, announced a new package text that he said all
parties could agree on, and said the conference would reconvene on Sunday
morning to approve the deal.

## 3. Hong Kong votes for its Election Committee

Polls opened at 9am on Sunday for the Election Committee Subsector Ordinary
Elections: 856 candidates competing for 723 seats across 31 subsectors. The
vote fills most of the 1,500-member Election Committee, the body that picks
Hong Kong's chief executive and elects 40 lawmakers.
